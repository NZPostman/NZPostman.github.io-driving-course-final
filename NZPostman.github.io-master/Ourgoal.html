Our Goal

Our goal is to create a driving course that reduces the risk of injury and death on NZ roads, therefore (hopefully) reducing the road toll. By teaching road users how to drive on the open road as 68.8% of all road fatalities in New Zealand are on the open road. The reason being is there's nothing in the road code or other requirements as part of getting a drivers licence. As well as improving the standard of driving in NZ. This driving course is an extension to what I believe the road code should include. Which is a course that helps you understand your limits, and your vehicle's limits. To reduce the risk of you injuring yourself or others.
